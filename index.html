about

Built in Ara, for students who want real skills, not just a certificate.

AI With Coding runs out of Tally Academy in Mahavir Tola, Ara — a home-ground institute that decided local students deserve the same depth of AI and software training usually reserved for metro cities.

Every course is designed around what employers and freelance clients are actually asking for right now: working Python, clean fundamentals, and enough AI/GenAI depth to build and ship real applications — not just recognise buzzwords in an interview.

Classes stay small, systems are set up for hands-on practice from day one, and every learner works toward a portfolio of real projects, not just completed chapters.
